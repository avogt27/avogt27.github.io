### Review: Getting Killed - Geese ###


-- album cover --

Man, this is the album I needed right now. There's something very fresh about music that is clearly real instruments played by real people recorded in real rooms. Not a lot of music being released right now  really captures the vibe of folks experimenting in the studio, looking for new sounds and laying down musical, nuanced performaces. At some point, I remember reading some old rock musician making the case that the first step to getting bands creating interesting music again would be for them to give up their electronic tuners, and although my tolerance for poor intonation is low, the sentiment rings true. With the proliferation of DAWs and the ability to endlessly polish music as it is being created, the aaesthetic trend seems to be towards keeping things as clean as possible, particularly when it comes to rhythm - with everyone playing to click tracks and even quantizing their parts to accurate, but markedly souless opinion of the metronome. I've always felt this approach is somewhat antithetical to rock music as a whole, at least to what personally draws me to certain recordings, and it's nice to see a band doing things differently to gain some traction among music lovers.
